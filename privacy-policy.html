Privacy Policy — MoneySetu

Effective date: 23 September 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneySetu ("MoneySetu", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the MoneySetu personal money manager application.

MoneySetu is designed as an offline-first personal finance tool for recording income, expenses, budgets, recurring items, split bills, notes, reminders, and related analysis.

2. Information stored by MoneySetu

MoneySetu can store the information that you choose to enter in the app, including:

  • Your display name and language preference.
  • Income and expense amounts, dates, categories, notes, and related transaction details.
  • Category names, icons, colors, and optional budgets.
  • Recurring income or expense settings, including frequency and next date.
  • Split-bill group names, member names, shared expenses, payment details, and settlement information.
  • In-app activity history, assistant chat/reminder data, and locally created backup data.

These details are stored locally on your device in the app/browser's local storage for the current MoneySetu implementation. MoneySetu does not require an account or a cloud database to use these features.

3. Information we collect or receive from you

MoneySetu does not, in its current implementation, send the above finance records to a MoneySetu server for account storage, advertising, or analytics.

We do not intentionally collect your bank account credentials, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, or camera data through the current application.

4. Third-party services and network requests

The current web application loads the jsPDF library from the cdnjs.cloudflare.com CDN to generate PDF reports. Loading that script requires a network request to the third-party CDN. As with ordinary internet requests, the CDN operator and network providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information, and request metadata.

MoneySetu does not send your locally stored transaction database to that CDN as part of generating a PDF. The PDF is generated in the app from data available on your device.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. MoneySetu is not responsible for independent processing by third-party providers outside our control.

5. Backups and data sharing

MoneySetu provides a user-initiated JSON backup/export feature. When you export a backup, the file is created on your device and downloaded or saved using the device/browser facilities you choose. When you restore a backup, the selected local file is read by the app and imported into local storage.

MoneySetu does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your locally stored financial records with advertisers or data brokers.

If you voluntarily share a backup file, screenshot, PDF, or other exported content with another person or service, that sharing is under your control and governed by the recipient's handling of the information.

6. Data security

MoneySetu uses local device/browser storage rather than a MoneySetu cloud database for its current data model. This means the security of locally stored data also depends on the security of your device, operating system, browser/app container, and device access controls.

Do not store extremely sensitive authentication secrets in MoneySetu. Use a strong device lock and keep backups in a secure location.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored MoneySetu data remains on your device until you delete it or the app/browser storage is cleared. You can remove data using the app's reset/delete functions, by clearing the app's storage/data, or by uninstalling the application, subject to the operating system's normal handling of app data and any backup files you created separately.

MoneySetu does not maintain a MoneySetu cloud account database for the current implementation, so there is no server-side account profile to delete through a web account portal.

If you have separately sent information to us by email or another support channel, we may retain that correspondence as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, comply with legal obligations, or maintain business records.

8. Children's privacy

MoneySetu is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for account creation, and the current app does not require an account.

9. Your choices

  • You choose what financial information to enter into MoneySetu.
  • You can edit or delete entries and locally stored finance records using the app's available controls.
  • You can choose whether to create or restore a backup file.
  • You can stop using the app and clear its local storage or uninstall it.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MoneySetu's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
